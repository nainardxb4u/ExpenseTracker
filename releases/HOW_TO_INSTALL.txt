Grocery Mate Android app
========================

File:    GroceryMate.apk
Package: com.nainar.grocerymate
Name:    Grocery Mate
Needs:   Android 7.0 or newer (works offline)

How to install
--------------
1. Copy GroceryMate.apk to your phone (Download, Drive, WhatsApp, USB, etc.).
2. Open the file on the phone.
3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
4. Tap Install, then Open.

How to use
----------
- Current List: type an item and tap Add. Check items you bought.
- Finish Shopping Trip: saves the list to History.
- History: past trips, or Clear History to delete them.

Your list and history stay on the phone.
